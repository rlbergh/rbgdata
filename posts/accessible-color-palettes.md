I'm excited to launch a new tool on RBG Data: the **Accessible Color Palette Helper**. Whether you're building a Tableau dashboard, designing a data visualization, or just want to make sure your colors work for everyone, this tool has you covered.

## What It Does

The Color Palette Helper lets you:

1. **Test Contrast Ratios** – See if your color combinations meet WCAG accessibility standards (AA or AAA levels)
2. **Simulate Color Blindness** – Preview how your palette appears to people with protanopia (red-blind), deuteranopia (green-blind), tritanopia (blue-blind), and achromatopsia (complete color blindness)
3. **Use Preset Palettes** – Jump-start with carefully curated accessible palettes like the RBG Data brand colors or accessible sequential scales
4. **Export for Tableau** – Copy your palette as comma-separated hex or RGB values, ready to paste into Tableau's custom palette feature

## Why This Matters

Color blindness affects roughly 8% of males and 0.5% of females worldwide. But even for people without color blindness, poor contrast makes visualizations hard to read on projectors or in print. Accessible color palettes aren't just inclusive—they're better for everyone.

## How to Use It

Head over to the [Tools](/tools/color-palette-helper) section and try it out:

- Pick a preset palette to see how it performs
- Or paste your own hex/RGB colors to test them
- Check contrast ratios between all color pairs
- See simulations of how different types of color blindness perceive your palette
- Export the results to use in Tableau

## Key Metrics to Know

- **WCAG AAA (7:1)** – Excellent contrast, works for all text sizes and audiences
- **WCAG AA (4.5:1)** – Minimum acceptable, better for larger text
- **Below 4.5:1** – May be hard to read; consider adjusting
